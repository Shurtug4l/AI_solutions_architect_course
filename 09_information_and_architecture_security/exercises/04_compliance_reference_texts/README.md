# Compliance reference texts

Course-provided readings from section 7 (compliance and regulations).

- `nis2_directive_eu_2022_2555.pdf`: Directive (EU) 2022/2555 (NIS 2), the EU-wide cybersecurity directive, full official text in Italian (73 pages). Covers scope (essential vs important entities), governance duties for management bodies, risk-management measures, and incident-notification deadlines.
- `belmont_report_1979.pdf`: the Belmont Report (1979), the foundational statement of research-ethics principles (respect for persons, beneficence, justice). Used in the section as the historical root of the ethics-by-design reasoning that the AI Act and modern AI governance frameworks build on.
